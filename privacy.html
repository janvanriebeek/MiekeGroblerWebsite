Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal information this website collects and how it is used.

What is collected

If you send a message through the contact form, the details you enter (your name, phone number, email address if given, and your message) are sent to Mieke Grobler. Nothing is collected unless you choose to send it.

How it is used

Your details are used only to reply to your enquiry and to arrange classes. They are never sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Services this website uses

The contact form is delivered by Formspree, which passes your message on by email. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These services may process technical information such as your IP address in order to work. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask to see, correct or delete the information held about you. To do so, email miekegrobler03@gmail.com.
