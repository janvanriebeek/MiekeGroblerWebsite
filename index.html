About Mieke

Hi, I'm Mieke Grobler, a passionate and dedicated teacher with 6 years of experience. I offer reading classes, and extra classes for learners from Grade 1 to Grade 12. My goal is to support each learner's unique journey by building strong foundations, improving confidence, and encouraging a love for learning.

I have extensive experience working with learners who have ADHD, dyslexia and autism. I understand the importance of a calm, supportive and flexible learning environment, and I use individualised strategies such as multi-sensory tools, step-by-step guidance and a steady routine to help every child succeed at their own pace.

Whether your child needs to catch up, prepare for exams, or wants ongoing enrichment, I'm here to help them reach their full potential.

  • Calm and supportiveA relaxed space where it is safe to ask questions and make mistakes.
  • Step by stepWork is broken into small, clear steps so nothing feels overwhelming.
  • Multi-sensoryHands-on tools that let learners see, hear and do, not only read.
  • Steady routineA predictable structure each lesson, at your child's own pace.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between reading classes and extra classes?

Reading classes focus on reading itself: reading ability, fluency, comprehension, vocabulary and confidence, in small groups, in Afrikaans and English. Extra classes help with schoolwork such as Maths, Maths Literacy, homework and test preparation, individually or in a small group.

How much do classes cost?

Reading classes are R399, R599 or R799 per month for 1, 2 or 3 sessions per week, plus a once-off R100 registration fee. Extra classes are R150 per hour per session.

What does the registration fee cover?

The R100 once-off fee applies to reading classes. It covers the initial reading assessment and setting up your child's material and programme.

My child has ADHD, dyslexia or autism. Can you help?

Yes. I have extensive experience with learners who have ADHD, dyslexia and autism, and I use a calm environment, multi-sensory tools, step-by-step guidance and a steady routine.

Where do classes take place?

Classes are in person in the Mossel Bay area. Get in touch and I'll send directions.

When are classes held?

Classes are held on Monday to Friday afternoons and Saturday mornings. Exact days and times are arranged with each family.

How does payment work?

Reading classes are paid monthly in advance and extra classes per session, by EFT or cash. Banking details are sent when you enrol.

What if my child misses a class?

Please let me know at least 24 hours ahead. With notice, we'll arrange a catch-up where possible.

How do I enrol my child?

Send a message by WhatsApp, email or the form below. Once we have agreed on the right class, download the enrolment form, fill it in and send it back by WhatsApp or email.
